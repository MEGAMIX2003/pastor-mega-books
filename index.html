About the author

Pastor Mega Philip Mega

Pastor Mega gave his life to Jesus Christ in 2010 and answered the call into ministry in 2018. After studying at Bible college (seminary), he was ordained three years later and has served as an assistant pastor.

He writes for everyday believers: the couples, parents, singles and seekers he meets in ministry. He is married to Gina, and they have three children, Mildred, Jeriel and Ailsa.

When you let the cross go before you… Jesus is Lord.

Questions

Before you buy

Will it open on my phone?

Yes. Every download includes a PDF, which opens on any phone, tablet or computer. On a phone, extract (unzip) the file first, then tap the PDF. The EPUB is there for e-reader apps such as Google Play Books or Apple Books.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by Selar. The payment methods shown depend on your country. Prices are set in CFA francs (XAF), and Selar can also show them in your currency.

When do I get the book?

Immediately after payment. The download link is also sent to your email, so you can download it again later.

Is this a printed book?

No, these are eBooks (digital downloads). Some titles are also available as paperbacks from online bookstores.

Which book should I start with?

If you're married or engaged, start with God's Blueprint for a Strong Christian Marriage. Parents: Bringing Up God-Fearing Children. Singles: Waiting Well or Dating on Purpose. For your own walk with God, start with the newest, The Driver.
